# Astra CMS demo videos

Twenty-two short videos recorded from the running demo: real Desk screens and real terminal output, no mock-ups.
Each video comes in English and Vietnamese. Videos 8 to 17 and 19 to 21 end with a "To be accurate" note on what
is not built yet, and Video 18 shows the roadmap, labelled "In development" on every frame.

*Hai mươi hai video quay từ bản demo đang chạy, mỗi video có bản tiếng Anh và tiếng Việt. Bấm ▶ Tiếng Việt để xem bản tiếng Việt.*

**Where to start:** the [Teaser](https://media.zerohostcms.com/astra-cms/astra-video-0-teaser-en.mp4) for the whole picture, then [Video 1](https://media.zerohostcms.com/astra-cms/astra-video-1-quickstart-en.mp4) to run it yourself.
Developers: Videos 2, 3, 9 and 14. Platform and security: Videos 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 16, 17 and 21.
Business processes: Videos 4, 5, 6, 19 and 20. AI agents over MCP: Video 15.

## Scripts and how to reproduce

- Scene-by-scene scripts, the command behind each scene, and the full "To be accurate" notes:
  [demo-video-series.en.md](../demo-video-series.en.md) · [demo-video-series.md](../demo-video-series.md) (tiếng Việt).
- Every scene is a real run of the demo stack: start it with `make k3d-up`, seed it with `make demo-seed`
  (see [Run the thin slice today](../../README.md#run-the-thin-slice-today)), then run the scene commands from the script.
- The videos are HyperFrames compositions kept outside the repository; only the narration text
  (`docs/video/*/narration*.json`) is versioned here. Voice-overs come from `scripts/video-voiceover.py`
  (VieNeu-TTS for Vietnamese, Kokoro for English).
